## Rust dependencies of the tailnet client (`net/ts`)

Built to `target/net/ts.wasm` by `net/ts/build.sh` (docs/tailnet.md), loaded only when
the user connects. Direct dependencies (`Cargo.lock` pins them, with their own; all permissive):
tailscale-rs's `ts_tunnel` (WireGuard, third-party audited), `ts_keys`, `ts_packet`, `ts_noise`
(the control server's Noise IK), `ts_capabilityversion`, `ts_control_serde` (the control protocol's
types), `ts_packetfilter`, `ts_packetfilter_state`, `ts_disco_protocol` v0.6.1 (BSD-3-Clause);
`h2`, `http`, `bytes`, `tokio` (its I/O traits only, no runtime; MIT) for HTTP/2 to the control
server; `crypto_box` (DERP's NaCl box), `chacha20poly1305`, `x25519-dalek`, `ed25519-dalek` (the
network-lock key), `ipnet`, `chrono`, `serde`, `serde_json`, `base64`, `getrandom` (MIT or Apache-2.0,
BSD-3-Clause for dalek). Through `ts_control_serde`: `url` and its IDNA tables (`idna`, ICU4X's
normalizer, MIT or Apache-2.0 and Unicode-3.0), `serde_with` (MIT or Apache-2.0). Through `ts_tunnel`: RustCrypto's `chacha20poly1305`, `blake2`, `hkdf`,
`x25519-dalek` (MIT or Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause for dalek), `tracing` (MIT), `zerocopy`
(BSD-2-Clause, Apache-2.0 or MIT).
