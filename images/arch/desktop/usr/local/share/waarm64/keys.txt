
  Keyboard shortcuts                      (F1 closes this again)

  Applications menu     Alt+F1 or Alt+F2 (or Super)
  Terminal              Ctrl+Alt+T
  Close a window        Alt+F4, or a middle click on its title
                        bar or its taskbar button
  Maximize / restore    Alt+F10, or a right click on its title
                        bar
  Minimize              Alt+F9, or click its taskbar button
  Next window           Alt+Tab  (Alt+Shift+Tab: the one before)
  Move a window         drag its title bar, or Alt+drag
  Resize a window       drag its edge, or Alt+right-drag

  Log out, shut down    Ctrl+Alt+Delete, or the taskbar's Power
  This sheet            F1, or the taskbar's Keys

  The taskbar: a window's button brings it to the front, or
  minimizes it when it is in front. The volume: click to mute,
  scroll to change it.

  Where the browser lets Super through, sway's own keys work
  too (Super+Enter, Super+Up / Down, ...).
  sway's settings: /etc/sway/config and
  /etc/sway/config.d/60-waarm64.conf (copy them to
  ~/.config/sway/config to change them).
